MY ARCHIVED DATA

📂 What is this?

To keep the app fast, an admin periodically moves very old duty entries out of active storage — this is called archiving. It's a routine housekeeping step, not something you did wrong, and it doesn't affect your recent data at all.

What you can do here: browse any of your own archived entries below, and regenerate a TA or NDA PDF for any archived month, exactly as it would have looked at the time. What you can't do: edit archived entries directly, or move them back into your active journal yourself — if you need that, ask your admin to restore that period.
